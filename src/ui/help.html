Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?

FinDeck supports Excel files (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV files (.csv). The maximum file size is 50MB. We're working on adding support for Google Sheets and other formats.

How long does the conversion process take?

Conversion time depends on file size and complexity. Simple files typically convert in 30-60 seconds, while complex files with multiple sheets and charts may take 2-5 minutes.

Can I customize the PowerPoint template?

Yes! You can choose from our pre-built templates or upload your own custom PowerPoint templates. Pro users can also set default templates for automatic application.

Is my data secure during conversion?

Absolutely. All files are encrypted during upload and processing. We don't store your files permanently - they're automatically deleted after 24 hours. We're SOC 2 compliant and follow industry-standard security practices.

What's the difference between Free and Pro plans?

Free plan includes 5 conversions per month with basic templates. Pro plan offers unlimited conversions, custom templates, priority processing, advanced formatting options, and priority support.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your account settings under "Billing & Plans". Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
