Q: How does the Excel to PPT converter work?

A: Upload your Excel file, select a template, and click “Convert.” Our system automatically detects charts, tables, and data to generate presentation-ready PowerPoint slides — formatted and editable.

Q: Can I use my own PowerPoint template?

A: Yes! You can upload your own company-branded template (available in Pro and AI plans). The converter will automatically fit your data into your design layout.

Q: Will my data be safe and private?

A: Absolutely. Your files are processed securely and deleted automatically after conversion. No one except you can access your uploaded data.

Q: What’s included in each plan?

A: Starter: 7 PPTs/month, standard templates Pro: 15 PPTs/month, premium templates, branding AI Power Plan: Unlimited PPTs, AI layouts, auto summaries

Is there a free trial?

A: Yes — you can try converting one presentation for free (with limited slides) before subscribing.

What is Power BI Dashboard Integration?

A: Power BI Integration transforms your Excel data into live, interactive dashboards with real-time sync. You get pre-built finance templates, embedded reports, and one-click PPT export. Available in BI Starter ($4,999/mo), BI Pro ($12,999/mo), and BI Enterprise (custom pricing). Perfect for teams needing dynamic dashboards + automated presentations.
